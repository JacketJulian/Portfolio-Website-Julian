## Skipper

Five real iOS Simulator recordings: home, Mallow customization, Rival Road, a simulated jump session, and accolades. Existing in-memory preview fixtures kept personal progress and saved data out of the capture. The video uses Skipper's cream, mint, teal, and rounded type styling. The gameplay chapter explicitly says it is simulated. Silent, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264, browser-compatible pixel format, and fast-start MP4.

## Bilingue

A real Chrome-extension dubbing session with the local inference server: Chinese source audio, English dubbed playback, translated subtitles, and a configured 30-second playback buffer. Captured browser audio only; microphone capture was disabled. The final edit excludes browser chrome, startup buffering, and a YouTube ad interruption. The visible prototype sometimes reports “Live with delays”; the recording preserves that status rather than implying flawless synchronization. 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264/AAC, browser-compatible pixel format, and fast-start MP4.

Source video credit appears in the Bilingue edit: **Andy and Sarah Mandarin**, [Learn Basic Chinese Phrases & Sentences for Beginners with a Story](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0OICvDnFW0w). This is a brief software demonstration excerpt, not original portfolio-owned footage. Review reuse permission before publicly publishing it; the capture does not establish a license from the creator.
